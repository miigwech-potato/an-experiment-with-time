# scoring

A hit is a pairing, not an entry. One entry may meet several events and one
event may meet several entries, so the unit recorded here is the pairing.

`entry_ids` takes one or more ids. `strength_0_3` is your own scale, written
down before you score anything: 0 nothing, 1 loose, 2 specific, 3 specific and
unlikely. `scored_blind` says whether you knew the arm when you scored.
`arm_revealed_later` stays empty until the mapping is published.

Score before the mapping is published. Once you know which arm an entry came
from, you cannot unknow it, and every score after that is a different kind of
evidence.
